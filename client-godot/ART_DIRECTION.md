# Isles of Mythos — Godot art direction

Target: visually rich stylized-realistic high-fantasy nautical worlds. Prioritise detailed tropical terrain, natural rock and foliage silhouettes, cohesive physically based materials, atmospheric lighting and believable wood/cloth/metal treatment of pirate ships. Avoid basic blocky low-poly pack styling as final art. Maintain mobile LOD and texture budgets driven by real measurements. Existing original generated meshes are scaffolding only.

## Visual quality direction

This scene is original untextured prototype geometry **only**. It is not the production art target. Final island, ship, creature and prop assets must have cohesive premium stylized-realistic PBR detail comparable to the approved lush island/pirate gameplay references, not simplistic low-poly asset-pack visuals. Asset admission requires explicit licence and source-provenance verification, stable content hashes and Android performance checks; no harvesting has been performed by this prototype.
